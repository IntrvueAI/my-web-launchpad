Intrvue.ai exists to make access to world-leading education more equitable by reducing the barriers pupils face during the preparation stage.

Leading independent schools invest hundreds of millions of pounds in bursaries each year. However, financial support at the point of admission does not address the unequal access to information, guidance and preparation that exists before a pupil applies.

Intrvue.ai provides pupils and families with high-quality, affordable preparation tools, while helping schools and community organisations deliver more effective support without placing additional pressure on already stretched staff.

Why we built this

An interview isn't a test.
It's a conversation.

Most children preparing for the 11+ only ever rehearse on paper. But the interview itself is spoken — a real conversation with a real adult, under real pressure. Confidence there doesn't come from memorising more answers. It comes from repetition: sitting the conversation itself, again and again, in real time, until it stops feeling like a test.
