A calm dashboard for people who are already nervous.
Same palette, type and card language as the coral Medicine landing page. Gamification is kept but demoted to a quiet detail. Below: the information architecture, then Home, a live station flow, and Feedback at desktop and mobile width, plus two proposed new tabs.
01 · Information architecture
Eight tabs became five, plus an account menu
Nothing is deleted — Questions folds into Practice as the question bank, Achievements folds into Progress. What's left in the nav is the five things a student opens on purpose.

ProgressNEW
Band trend over time, per-skill and per-station-type movement. Absorbs Achievements as a milestones strip.
SchoolsNEW
The published MMI-format data brought inside the product: prep time, station count, timing — and "practise this exact format".
FeedbackREDESIGN
Past sessions and their written, banded feedback. Renamed from "history" everywhere in the UI.
Moved into the account menu
Credits & billing Settings Grown-up view Interview diary · new
Credits stay one tap away as a live balance pill in the header, so the tab isn't needed. Interview diary is a proposal: the real school dates a student enters once, which then drive Home's countdown, Schools' format card and the parent view.
11+ and Medicine
Rather than two parallel navigations, the product line is a single switch in the header. It changes vocabulary (stations vs. subjects), the Practice catalogue, the Schools dataset and the feedback rubric — the shell stays identical. A household using both sees one switch, not two accounts.
